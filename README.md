# 🏙️ City Grievance Services

A web-based **City Grievance Management System** that provides a centralized platform for citizens to register, track, and manage civic complaints related to public services such as roads, water supply, sanitation, and other municipal issues.

The system helps citizens communicate their grievances to the concerned authorities while providing administrators and departments with tools to review, assign, monitor, and resolve complaints efficiently.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

City Grievance Services is designed to digitize the traditional grievance registration and management process.

Instead of citizens visiting municipal offices or manually following up on complaints, the system provides an online platform where users can:

- Register and log in securely
- Submit civic complaints
- Select complaint categories
- Provide complaint descriptions
- Add location information
- Upload supporting images/documents
- Track complaint status
- View complaint history
- Provide feedback after resolution

Administrators can use the system to:

- View registered complaints
- Review submitted grievances
- Assign complaints to appropriate departments
- Update complaint status
- Add remarks
- Monitor complaint progress
- Manage users
- Track complaint resolution
- Generate reports

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The primary objectives of City Grievance Services are:

1. Provide citizens with an easy-to-use platform for reporting civic issues.
2. Digitize the grievance registration and tracking process.
3. Reduce manual paperwork and administrative effort.
4. Improve communication between citizens and municipal departments.
5. Provide transparency through complaint status tracking.
6. Enable authorities to efficiently assign and manage grievances.
7. Maintain a centralized database of complaints and their resolution history.
8. Improve accountability and response management.

# 🚀 Key Features

## 👤 Citizen Module

### Registration and Login

Citizens can create an account and securely access the grievance management system.

Features include:

- User registration
- Login authentication
- Session management
- User profile management
- Logout functionality

---

### 📝 Complaint Registration

Citizens can submit complaints by providing relevant information.

A complaint may contain:

- Complaint title
- Complaint category
- Detailed description
- Location
- Date
- Supporting image/document
- Contact information

---

### 🏷️ Complaint Categories

Complaints can be organized into different civic-service categories such as:

- 🚰 Water Supply
- 🛣️ Roads and Potholes
- 🗑️ Garbage and Sanitation
- 💡 Street Lights
- 🚽 Public Sanitation
- 🌳 Parks and Public Spaces
- 🏢 Municipal Services
- ⚠️ Other Civic Issues

---
